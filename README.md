# Iris

Unlock your Mac's screen by looking at it and blinking once.

Iris is a small menu-bar app for macOS. It covers the screen until its camera check recognises your face and sees you blink.

## Get it

Download **Iris.dmg** from the [latest release](https://github.com/littleme723/iris/releases/latest), drag Iris to Applications and open it from there. A setup guide, "How to set up Iris.html", is inside the disk image.

Iris needs macOS 14 or later and a Mac with a camera.

The first time you open it, macOS says it cannot verify the app, because Iris is not registered with Apple. The setup guide explains how to open it.

## Good to know

- Iris is a convenience, not a security system. It is an app that covers your screen, not the Mac's own lock, and it does not replace your password.
- Your face is stored on your Mac as 128 numbers, never as a photo. Nothing about you or your face is sent anywhere.
- Once a day Iris checks this page for a newer version and installs it by itself. Updates are accepted only if they carry the developer's signature. You can switch this off in the Iris window.
